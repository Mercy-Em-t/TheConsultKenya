# Scenario & Jackson-Zave Specification: UC-07

### UC-07: Attach Conversion Anchor Call-to-Action
* **Requirement ($R_{07}$):** Every published case study must serve as an active top-of-funnel conversion vehicle, directly driving client discovery calls and paid advisory contracts.
* **Specification ($S_{07}$):**
  * The machine shall support 4 native anchor types: *15-Min Discovery Call*, *Technical Audit Inquiry*, *Download Framework*, and *Retainer Inquiry*.
  * The machine shall scan CTA input fields and reject any submission containing external phone numbers, PayPal links, or bypass URLs.
  * The machine shall bind the anchor directly to the consultant’s live booking calendar or escrow inquiry modal.
* **Assumption ($E_{07}$):**
  * Direct in-context conversion hooks capture client commercial intent at peak engagement when they finish reading a compelling deliverable.
* **Narrative Scenario ($SC_{07}$):**
  * *Context:* A fintech founder finishes reading Kiprono’s API security case study on Slide 6.
  * *Execution:* Immediately beneath the final slide, a high-contrast pill button reads: "Request 30-Min Security Audit (KES 20,000)". The founder taps the button, and the booking sheet slides up instantly over the post without navigating away.
