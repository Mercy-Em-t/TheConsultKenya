# Scenario & Jackson-Zave Specification: UC-19

### UC-19: Manage Phased Rollout & Creator Onboarding
* **Requirement ($R_{19}$):** The platform must transition smoothly from a proprietary agency to a curated beta and an open ecosystem without technical re-architecture or quality dilution.
* **Specification ($S_{19}$):**
  * In Phase 1, the machine shall lock publishing exclusively to proprietary agency accounts.
  * In Phase 2, the machine shall gate creator registration behind cryptographic invitation codes issued to selected peers.
  * In Phase 3, the machine shall open automated self-service onboarding with multi-tenant workspace isolation.
* **Assumption ($E_{19}$):**
  * Launching as an agency first primes the content engine and proves unit economics before expanding to a multi-sided marketplace.
* **Narrative Scenario ($SC_{19}$):**
  * *Context:* The platform founder decides it is time to move from Phase 1 to Phase 2.
  * *Execution:* The admin toggles the global rollout state to "Phase 2: Curated Cohort" and generates 25 invite codes. Selected peer specialists redeem the codes, receive "Founding Creator" badges, and begin populating the feed with peer-reviewed engineering case studies.
