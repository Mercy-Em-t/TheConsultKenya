# Scenario & Jackson-Zave Specification: UC-02

### UC-02: Inspect Consultant Portfolio (Bento Grid)
* **Requirement ($R_{02}$):** Prospective clients must be able to audit a consultant's competence, verified past deliverables, and credential status within 5 seconds of opening their profile.
* **Specification ($S_{02}$):**
  * The machine shall render a multi-tile Bento Grid above the fold containing: (1) Top 3 flagship case study tiles, (2) verified statutory badge, (3) aggregate client star rating, and (4) sticky booking CTA.
  * The machine shall provide a contextual sub-toggle allowing the user to switch between "Bento Grid" and "Chronological Feed" without a page refresh or losing scroll position.
* **Assumption ($E_{02}$):**
  * Enterprise clients make initial procurement shortlisting decisions based on visual evidence of past work rather than reading comprehensive text CVs.
* **Narrative Scenario ($SC_{02}$):**
  * *Context:* Sarah, an enterprise procurement director at an insurance firm, taps on Leah’s avatar after seeing her FinTech compliance deck.
  * *Execution:* Within 2 seconds, Leah’s Bento Grid loads. Sarah immediately sees Leah's Gold Verified badge, her LSK bar registration, her average rating of 4.9/5 across 18 completed escrow contracts, and three clickable visual architecture blueprints. Within 4 seconds, Sarah has verified Leah's domain authority and taps the sticky "Book Consultation" button.
