# System Message Specification: MSG-WARNING-01
**Message Classification:** Warning Message

### When Displayed (Trigger)
Approaching critical thresholds (e.g., 72-hour inspection timer expiring in 6 hours, calendar slot hold expiring in 2 minutes).

### Visual Presentation & Styling
Amber alert banner (#D97706) pinned to top of viewport with countdown clock icon.

### Canonical Copy & Text
> **"Your 15-minute slot lock will expire in 2:00 minutes. Please complete payment to confirm your booking."**

### Dismissal & User Action Protocol
Remains sticky until action is completed or timer expires.
