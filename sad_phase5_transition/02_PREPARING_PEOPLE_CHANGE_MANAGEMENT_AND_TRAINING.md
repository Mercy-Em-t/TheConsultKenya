## 1. Change Management Roles

| Role | Who at Consult Kenya | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sponsor** | Founder / CEO | Wants the change, funds it, speaks for it publicly |
| **Change Agent** | Head of Community & Onboarding plus 2 concierge onboarding staff | Leads the change day to day; trains; collects feedback |
| **Potential Adopters** | Consultants (advocates, CPAs, engineers, specialist advisors), SME clients, internal staff (Finance, Compliance, Mediators, Support) | Must actually change how they work |

> Adoption is voluntary for consultants and clients, since they are not our employees. Our levers are **persuasion, incentives and ease**, not instructions. Policies only bind internal staff, plus users through the Terms of Service.

## 2. Understanding Resistance to Change

Dennis-Wixom: people resist when **the change costs them more than it benefits them**, as they see it, and when they doubt the change will work. Resistance is rational from where they stand. The job is to understand it, not overrule it.

### 2.1 Resistance Map by Stakeholder

| Stakeholder | Likely Resistance | Root Cause | Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Senior advocates (LSK)** | "Advocates don't advertise"; worry about the Advocates (Practice) Rules on advertising and touting | Professional-ethics risk; status | Get an LSK ethics opinion before the pilot; frame content as *thought-leadership case studies*, not ads; no fee-comparison ranking for advocates |
| **CPAs (ICPAK)** | "My clients already know me"; dislike of 7% commission | Commission cost; channel already works | Commission only on platform-sourced clients; show cost of unpaid invoices; eTIMS + WHT done automatically saves admin time |
| **Engineers (EBK)** | Low digital content habit; "I'm not a writer" | Effort to create case studies | Studio templates (PROG-02); concierge writes the first case study from a 20-minute interview |
| **All consultants** | Fear of public bad reviews and disputes; fear funds get stuck in escrow | Loss of control over reputation and cash flow | Clear 72 h auto-approval rule; dispute process explained up front; payout times published from real data |
| **SME clients** | "Why pay into escrow, not directly?"; distrust of new platforms holding money | Trust; unfamiliarity | Escrow explained in plain English/Kiswahili; regulated-trust-account statement; small first-session voucher |
| **Clients used to informal deals** | Lose ability to negotiate cash or skip invoices/WHT | Tax visibility | Be honest: compliance is a feature (eTIMS invoices are claimable expenses for the client's own tax) |
| **Internal Finance staff** | New ledger, reconciliation duties; fear of being blamed for errors | New skills; accountability | Early involvement in reconciliation design; parallel manual reconciliation in pilot; clear runbooks |
| **Mediators** | Unclear authority and liability for rulings | Legal exposure | Written mediator charter; professional indemnity; ruling templates |

### 2.2 Perceived Cost vs Benefit (Adopter's View)

| Adopter | Perceived Costs | Perceived Benefits | Net (before intervention) | Intervention to Improve |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| Consultant | 7% commission, time to build profile, learning curve, review exposure | New clients, guaranteed payment, automatic eTIMS/WHT, authority badge | Uncertain | Pilot fee waiver; concierge profile setup; publish real payout timings |
| SME client | Learning a new tool, escrow unfamiliarity | Verified experts, payment protection, compliant invoices | Positive once trust exists | Trust signals (verification badges, escrow explainer), first-session voucher |
| Finance staff | New processes, more reconciliation | Less manual invoicing, audit trail | Slightly negative at first | Training, runbooks, automated reconciliation report |
| Compliance officer | Manual review queue during registry outages | Fewer fake credentials | Positive | Clear queue SLAs |

## 4. Revising Management Policies

Policies are the rules that make the new system the normal way of working (Lewin's "refreeze").

| # | Policy | Applies To | Change |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| P1 | **Escrow-only payment policy** | Users (ToS) | Payments for engagements booked through the platform must go through escrow. Off-platform payment for platform-sourced clients during a 12-month window breaches ToS. |
| P2 | **Credential verification policy** | Consultants | Only `VERIFIED_ACTIVE` consultants appear in discovery for regulated professions. Annual re-verification when practising certificates renew (LSK and ICPAK renew yearly). |
| P3 | **Content & advertising ethics policy** | Advocates especially | Case studies anonymise clients and follow LSK guidance; moderation queue (UC-20) before publication. |
| P4 | **Dispute & refund policy** | All users | 7-day filing window after delivery; 48 h rebuttal; mediator ruling is final on the platform; does not remove legal rights. |
| P5 | **Data protection & retention policy** | Staff, users | KDPA 2019: lawful basis, consent for recording sessions, retention periods (ledger 7 years for KRA; session recordings 90 days unless disputed), data subject rights handling within 30 days. |
| P6 | **Financial control policy** | Finance | Daily reconciliation; two-person approval for manual payouts above KES 100,000; payout freeze on any ledger imbalance. |
| P7 | **Staff performance measures** | Internal | Support: first-response time. Compliance: verification turnaround ≤ 24 h. Finance: reconciliation closed daily. Onboarding: activated consultants per week. |
| P8 | **Change request policy** | Staff | All system changes go through the change request process (doc 03 §2); no direct production edits. |

## 5. Motivating Adoption

Dennis-Wixom gives two strategies. Consult Kenya uses both, aimed at different groups.

### 5.1 Informational Strategy — *show them the benefits*

For people who will adopt once they see the value: most clients, younger consultants, tech-comfortable CPAs.

- **Evidence-led messaging:** real pilot numbers (sessions completed, payout times) once available. No made-up statistics.
- **Explainer content:** 60-second videos in English and Kiswahili: "How escrow protects you", "How your eTIMS invoice is created".
- **Demos at professional-body events:** LSK branch CPD sessions, ICPAK regional forums, EBK seminars.
- **Peer testimonials** from pilot consultants (with consent).

### 5.2 Political Strategy — *use influence and incentives*

For people who resist or need a push: senior advocates and established firms.

- **Opinion leaders first:** recruit 3–5 respected practitioners per profession into the pilot as "Founding Authorities" (TIER_3 badge, featured placement).
- **Institutional endorsement:** seek a partnership or CPD recognition with ICPAK/EBK; ethics clearance from LSK.
- **Incentives:** commission waived for first 3 months or first 10 sessions; client first-session voucher.
- **Network effect:** once clients ask for "book me on Consult Kenya", consultants follow. So seed demand through SME associations (KAM, KEPSA SME network, county chambers of commerce).

### 5.3 Adopter Segmentation (Rogers-style, for targeting)

| Segment | Who | Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Innovators / early adopters | Young CPAs, tech-law advocates, Nairobi startups | Informational; pilot invitation |
| Early majority | Mid-career professionals, established SMEs | Informational + peer proof |
| Late majority / resisters | Senior advocates, traditional firms | Political: opinion leaders, endorsements |

## 6. Enabling Adoption

Motivation is not enough; people also need to be *able* to use the system.

| Enabler | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Concierge onboarding** | Staff set up the first profile and case study with the consultant (20–45 min call) |
| **Low-bandwidth design** | Mobile web on 3G; lightweight feed; audio-only fallback for sessions |
| **Language** | UI and help in English and Kiswahili |
| **In-app help** | Context help bubbles; message catalogue from Phase 3 UI design (`sad_phase3_ui_design/messages/`) |
| **Help desk** | WhatsApp Business line, email, in-app chat; 08:00–20:00 EAT during pilot |
| **Documentation** | Phase 4 Reference, Procedure Manual and Tutorials, after correcting the parameter mismatch |
| **Sandbox / practice mode** | Consultants can do a test booking with a staff "client" using KES 1 STK |

## 7. Training

### 7.1 What to Train — Focus on Tasks, Not Features

| Audience | Training Content (task-based) | Depth |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Consultants** | Create profile → submit credentials → build case study in Studio → set availability → run a session → submit deliverable → read payout & eTIMS invoice → respond to a dispute | Core tasks only |
| **SME clients** | Search/feed → assess a consultant (badges, case studies) → book & pay via M-Pesa → join session → approve deliverable or raise a dispute | Very light, mostly in-product |
| **Finance staff** | Ledger structure; daily reconciliation against Daraja statements; manual payout fallback (C1/C4); eTIMS queue monitoring; WHT certificates | Deep |
| **Compliance officers** | Verification queue; manual registry checks (C6); content moderation | Deep |
| **Mediators** | Dispute console; evidence review; ruling with split; certificate | Deep, plus a mediator charter |
| **Support staff** | All user tasks + troubleshooting + escalation + contingency procedures | Deepest (system-wide) |
| **DevOps / developers** | Runbooks, feature flags, rollback, incident response, change request process | Technical |

### 7.2 How to Train — Methods Chosen

| Method | Used For | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Classroom / workshop (in person)** | Finance, Compliance, Mediators, Support; pilot "Founding Authorities" | High-stakes tasks; questions answered live |
| **One-on-one (concierge)** | Senior consultants | Their time is costly and status matters; personal setup removes the effort barrier |
| **Computer-based training (short videos, 2–4 min)** | All consultants and clients, at scale for Phases A–D | Cheap to scale; self-paced; watchable on phones |
| **Embedded in-product help** | Clients | Clients won't attend training; the product has to teach them |
| **Webinars** | Regional rollout (Mombasa, Kisumu, others) | Reach without travel |
| **Train-the-trainer** | Professional-body chapter champions | Multiplies reach through trusted peers |

### 7.3 Training Schedule (aligned to doc 01 waves)

| When | Who | Format |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Before M1 | Support, Compliance, Onboarding staff | 2-day workshop |
| Before M3 | Finance, Mediators | 2-day workshop + reconciliation dry run with KES 1 transactions |
| Pilot week 0 | 30 pilot consultants | 1:1 concierge + 90-min group session |
| Pilot onward | Clients | In-product + videos |
| Each phase | Regional consultants | Webinar + chapter event + videos |

### 7.4 Training Evaluation

Measure whether training worked:
- Task completion without help (first booking, first deliverable) from product analytics.
- Support tickets per active user, by topic. Topics that keep recurring become new training content.
- Short post-training quiz for staff (≥ 80% pass for Finance/Mediators before go-live).
